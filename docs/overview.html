JARVIS HUD

A voice assistant that lives in your Mac's terminal. Say "hey Jarvis" or hold Space, and it opens apps, runs your calendar and reminders, plans your day and answers in a British voice you can interrupt.

HOST Apple M4 · 16 GB OS macOS 26.6 RUNTIME Python 3.12 (uv) REPO jarvis-hud · public

How a command travels

Wake word / Space→Whisper (on-device)→Fast router <15 ms→Mac action or Claude→Spoken reply

Simple commands like "open Safari" or "what's on the agenda today" never leave your Mac. Anything that needs thinking, like "plan my day", goes to Claude, which can create events and reminders through the same safety layer.

What Jarvis may do

LevelActions
Runs nowOpen and quit apps, volume, brightness, music, timers, system status, read today's agenda, create events and reminders, plan the day
Asks firstDelete or move files, delete or reschedule events, delete reminders, shell commands, send messages. Jarvis reads the action back; say "yes" or press Enter. Silence for 10 s cancels.
Neversudo, Keychain, password managers, disk erase, and any file holding keys: .env, ~/.ssh, *.pem, tokens

Every word sent to Claude, written to the log or spoken aloud is scrubbed for keys and passwords first. These rules live in code, so a clever prompt can't talk Jarvis out of them.

Agenda & planning

"What's on the agenda today" reads your events and due reminders instantly from Apple Calendar and Reminders, with no cloud call.

"Plan my day" creates everything straight away, gives a short summary, then asks:

"Tell me if I missed anything, or if there's something you'd like to add."

The mic stays open for 8 seconds so you can answer without the wake word.

Personality

A bank of original lines, at least 8 per moment, so Jarvis never repeats itself back to back:

  • Waking up, done, failed, cancelled
  • Morning, afternoon and evening greetings
  • Agenda intro and empty-day lines
  • Low battery and high CPU heads-ups
  • "Anything I missed?" after any multi-step task

All lines live in prompts/lines.yaml, so you can add your own.

The screen

A full-terminal display with a 3D dot sphere in the centre that spins faster as you speak and pulses while Jarvis talks. It turns amber when Jarvis is waiting for a confirmation and red on errors. Live CPU, memory, battery, network and git branch run down the left. An aerospace-style action log runs down the right, with your last words fading along the bottom.

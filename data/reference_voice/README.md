# Reference voice

To enable voice cloning, drop two files in this folder:

- `jarvis_ref.wav` - a clean mono recording (about 10-30 s)
- `jarvis_ref.txt` - the exact transcript of that recording

The voice must be your own or one you hold a licence to use. Do not use recordings of real people or film characters without permission. Both files are git-ignored. Without them, Jarvis uses the built-in Kokoro voice.
